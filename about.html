About Fast Mode

Fast Mode is a free tool that measures your internet connection from your browser: download speed, upload speed, ping and jitter, plus a DNS benchmark that compares public DNS providers over DNS-over-HTTPS.

There is nothing to install and no account to create. The site is funded by affiliate links and may show advertising in the future.

How accurate is it?

Browser-based tests are very good at showing what your connection can do in practice, but they are influenced by your device, Wi-Fi, browser extensions and distance to the test server. See how the test works for details.
